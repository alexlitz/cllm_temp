### Important: xfail semantics

Every `test_program` carries `@pytest.mark.xfail(strict=False)` (Phase 8
baseline marker). So in pytest terms:
- **`X` (XPASS)** = the program **PASSED** in pure-neural mode (neural
  exit_code == declarative exit_code). These are the real passes.
- **`x` (xfail)** = the program **FAILED** (neural diverged from
  declarative, or compile/declarative error).
- No hard `F`/`E` and no `s` skips were produced — the batched fixture
  caught all per-chunk exceptions into `err` strings (counted as xfail).

## Where the 62 passes live (XPASS positions)

The XPASS (`X`) markers cluster heavily in the **first ~250 programs**
(add/sub/mul/div/mod, indices 0-249) and a band around indices ~700-790
(the `func_*` / early `rec_*` interleave region from the progress bar).
Concretely the passing mass is:
- A subset of **add/sub** (clean single-byte results, no hi-byte carry).
- Scattered **mul/div/mod** that happen to produce single-byte results
  (no 16-bit cascade, no ADDR_KEY collision).
- A few **func_identity / edge / bool_and** short programs.

Everything that requires (a) a stack-relative MEM store (`int x = N`),
(b) a 16-bit ALU high-byte, (c) CMP-driven control flow with operand
comparison, or (d) JSR/LEV PC restore — fails.

## Cross-reference with smoke (downstream of in-flight smoke fixes)

Per `project_operand_gather_hybrid_encoding_is_cmp_alu_root`, three
independent agents converged: **the operand-gather (block 8) hybrid
magnitude+nibble encoding** is the shared wall behind ~10 smoke failures
(eq/and/mul/16-bit-bitwise). That same wall is the dominant 1096 blocker:

- **Clusters C, D, H, I, K, L (~400 programs)** are downstream of the
  **operand-gather one-hot / CMP** smoke fix. Any clean-one-hot fix to
  block 8 would unblock EQ/NE/LT/GT comparison and multi-byte ALU at once
  — the single highest-leverage 1096 lever. (High risk: block-8 +
  block-11 heads 3/4 are load-bearing for PSH/STACK0/byte-passthrough.)
- **Clusters B, G, J (~250 programs)** are downstream of the **JSR/ENT/LEV**
  smoke fix (`test_simple_function` arch-blocked). Needs L10 PC
  byte_passthrough head + real `_set_layer9_lev_bp_to_pc_relay` — multi-commit.
- **Cluster D (mul/div/mod, 150 programs)** is downstream of the
  **MUL/DIV/MOD** smoke cluster (per-nibble div wrongness, width=2<->ADDR_KEY
  collision, `OP_MUL` not reaching AX). Multi-commit per
  `project_mul_div_mod_arch_blocked`.
- **Cluster A (var_*, 100 programs)** is the stack-relative MEM-store
  baseline — not a smoke test directly, but the failure mode has shifted
  L15->L3->L10->L34 across sessions; **re-attribute on current HEAD before
  any fix** (`project_var_failure_mode_shifted`). It is the prerequisite
  for B/F/G/J too (every function/loop/recursion program does an
  `int x = N` stack store in its prologue).

### Net "blocked-on-smoke" accounting

Roughly **~700 of the ~1034 failures** (clusters B, C, D, G, H, I, J, K, L)
are downstream of one of the four in-flight smoke surfaces. Landing the
operand-gather clean-one-hot fix + JSR/LEV PC-restore would be expected to
move the largest mass. The var-store baseline (A) gates the
function/loop/recursion clusters and should be re-attributed first.
